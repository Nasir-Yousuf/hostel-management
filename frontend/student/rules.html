NIYD Residential Code of Conduct & Rules

Official Policy

1. Gate Entry & Curfew Schedule

• Hostel gates are unlocked at 06:00 AM and strictly secured at 09:30 PM daily.
• Any student arriving after 09:30 PM must register in the Late Entry Book and provide written justification. Chronic late returns incur disciplinary fines.

2. Visitor & Guardian Policy

• External visitors and family guardians may be received solely in the ground floor visitor lounge between 04:00 PM and 07:00 PM.
• No outside visitors or non-resident guests are permitted in residential student rooms at any hour.

3. Electrical Appliance & Safety Guidelines

• High-wattage heating coils, electric stoves, and unauthorized room heaters are strictly prohibited due to fire hazards.
• Study tables are equipped with authorized power sockets for laptops and phone chargers.

4. Maintenance & Property Care

• Residents are held jointly responsible for room fixtures, study desks, and sanitary fittings.
• Any accidental or structural damage must be promptly logged via the portal's Maintenance Request system.

5. Disciplinary Actions

• Smoking, narcotics, alcohol, weapons, or harassment of fellow students will result in immediate termination of hostel accommodation and referral to the Academic Disciplinary Board.
