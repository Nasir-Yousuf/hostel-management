Residential Guidelines

Rules & Guidelines

To maintain discipline and mutual respect, all hostel residents must abide by these institutional guidelines.

Entry & Exit Timings

Hostel gates open at 6:00 AM and close strictly at 9:30 PM. Any late arrivals must be recorded in the late register with prior assistant warden approval.

Visitor Policy

External visitors and guardians may only meet residents in the designated ground floor visitor lounge between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM. No unauthorized guests are allowed inside resident rooms.

Safety, Discipline & Prohibited Items

Smoking, narcotics, firearms, and high-wattage unapproved electric appliances (heaters/hotplates) are strictly prohibited and result in immediate disciplinary suspension.
